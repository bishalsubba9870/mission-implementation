==============================================================================
OPERATIONAL DEFINITIONS
==============================================================================
Modification Cost:
  added normalized source lines + deleted normalized source lines
  between consecutive scenario snapshots.

Change Propagation:
  changed formalism-specific files / total formalism-specific files
  involved in the compared snapshots.

Reactivity Cost:
  nominal-to-final Delta SLOC, Delta Radon blocks, and direct edit cost.

Recovery Locality:
  number of formalism-specific files and functions/methods that contain
  scenario/recovery event knowledge in the final snapshot.

Reuse Evidence:
  similarity ratio between normalized nominal and final
  formalism-specific source lines.

Behavioral Coverage:
  supported required runtime scenarios / 6.
